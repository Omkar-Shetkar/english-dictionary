How to enable local PDF lookups (file:/// URLs):

  • Google Chrome / Chromium: Open chrome://extensions, click Details on Simple English Dictionary, and toggle on "Allow access to file URLs".
  • Mozilla Firefox: Web PDFs work immediately. For local files, open about:addons, click Simple English Dictionary, and ensure file permissions are granted under Details.
  • Right-Click Context Menu: Select any word or phrase in a PDF, right-click, and choose "Look up in Simple English Dictionary".

How to Use

  • Single Words: Double-click any word or abbreviation (AI, photosynthesis, amortization) to view definitions.
  • Compound Concepts: Highlight 2 to 4 words (machine learning, compound interest) and click the floating dictionary icon.
  • Modifier Shortcut: Hold your selected trigger key (Alt, Ctrl, or Shift) while selecting to open popup immediately on mouse release.
  • PDF Documents & Right-Click: Select any word in a web or local PDF, right-click, and select "Look up in Simple English Dictionary" for an instant definition.
